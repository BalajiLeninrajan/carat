Screenshots and display

Off unless you turn it on. When on, and a tab you are reading is mostly an image, a pasted screenshot or a canvas app, Carat takes one picture of that tab while it is in front and keeps it in session memory for at most three minutes, two pictures at most. When you switch away, the picture goes to the smart model, which writes down the text it can see plus a few facts it works out from the picture (dates resolved to the calendar, places, addresses, prices); the picture is deleted and only that text is kept, for 30 minutes like everything else. No picture is ever taken of a page Carat has offered to fill, of a page with a password field, or of a page on the denylist.

A small line in the bottom-left corner of every page: whether Carat is running there and which model answers. Off by default.
