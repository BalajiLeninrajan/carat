Carat

Cursor-style autocomplete for the browser, grounded in Chrome's accessibility tree. Tap right Shift to accept, Esc to dismiss, Ctrl+→ for one word.

When you leave a page, Carat asks the model to note the facts on it you might act on elsewhere, keeps them for an hour, and includes them in later prompts. The page's visible text is sent to the API to do this. Skipped on blocklisted sites and pages with password fields.
