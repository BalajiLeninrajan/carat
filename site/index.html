Two spellings on record — asking you Guan / Kwan

The refusal is the feature. A naïve agent picks the newer source and types a surname that may be wrong onto a ticket that cannot be edited. Caret fills the half the sources agree on, marks the other half disputed, and hands that one decision back to you — because a wrong name on a rail booking costs more than a question does.

Architecture

One loop, two clocks.

Reading and acting both go through CDP. Everything the agent learns lands in Elasticsearch, and everything it decides is shaped by what it reads back out — including closing out the errand it just finished.

How it reads a page

Structure, not soup.

A DOM scrape gives you markup. The accessibility tree gives you what the page means — every control with its role, its name and its state, which is exactly what deciding on an action needs.

⇥

Next-action chips

Click, fill, select, submit, scroll, switch tab, or open a destination. Grounded in the page, your notes and what you just did — never invented.

✎

Ghost text

Pause mid-sentence and the rest of the line appears in grey after the caret. Tab takes it, Ctrl+→ takes a single word.

☵

Reading memory

Facts from pages you've left, kept for an hour in session storage and offered on whatever site can act on them. Opt-in.

◫

Conflict-aware

Two sources disagreeing about a time or a surname isn't a crash. It's a flagged conflict, and Caret declines to fill the disputed detail.

▣

Debug panel

Alt+Shift+D shows the request, the outline it sent, the answer, token counts and latency for the current tab.

⚊

Off where it matters

Banks, identity providers and password managers are never touched, whatever your own blocklist says. Password and one-time-code fields are skipped.

Elastic — Find the Signal

Elasticsearch as the agent's context layer.

Not retrieval bolted onto a chatbot. The agent decides which queries to run based on what the page in front of you can actually do, and closes tasks out when you accept a suggestion.

IndexHolds
caret-observationsThe accessibility outline of every page read
caret-factsFacts distilled from pages you've left behind
caret-tasksOpen errands, with the fields still to enter
caret-detailsWho people are — name parts, email, phone — keyed by who it's about
caret-actionsChips accepted and dismissed

Hybrid retrieval

RRF over BM25 and semantic_text when an inference endpoint is set, BM25 alone when it isn't. Three queries in parallel under one 1.8 s deadline.

ES|QL rollup

STATS … BY status, actionType counts what's still open — and only runs when this page can finish something.

Ingest pipeline

Every write is stamped with received_at, normalised host and origin, ECS-style event.* metadata, and card-like digit runs redacted before the document lands.

Two clocks

Tasks are hot intent — a five-minute window, swept continuously. Context is long memory — twelve hours. A task and a fact age differently, so they're queried differently.

What reaches the model

[task] calendar_event from discord.com: Dinner at Seven Shores Cafe on Friday at 6. — still to enter: when="Friday at 6", location="Seven Shores Cafe" [elasticsearch] ES|QL task summary: 1 unresolved calendar_event task open in the last 5 minutes [elasticsearch] page from discord.com, 2026-09-20T04:41Z: … [task] personal_detail for Pez Kwan — conflict: family_name has been given as Guan / Kwan, so do not fill it

Rox — Best AI Agent

Real data is a mess. That's the job.

≠

Conflicting sources

One source says dinner at 6, another says 7. The task keeps a single document, flips to conflict, and the disagreement travels with the record instead of beside it.

⚇

Entity resolution

“Pez Guan” and “Pez Kwan” resolve to one person with a disputed surname, not two people — identity keys on the given name.

☸

Whose detail is it?

“My name is X” is you. “Is your name X”, said to you, is someone else — likely who you're booking for. Caret files them apart and lets the form's own labels decide.

↻

Incomplete by default

A task is the fields the destination will ask for. Each fill completes one; only the last closes it. Travelling to a page completes nothing, so the errand survives the journey.

⚠

Declines under doubt

When two people could fill the same form and nothing settles which, Caret fills neither and picks a different action rather than guessing whose name goes in the box.

✓

Closes the loop

Accepting a chip isn't just an answer — it's a write. The action is recorded and the task it belonged to is advanced or deleted.
